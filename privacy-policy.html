MyLab Privacy Policy

MyLab — Laboratory Information System for Windows and Android. Last updated: 18 October 2026.

MyLab is software that a laboratory installs and operates on its own computers to record patients, test orders, results, reports and bills. This policy explains what data MyLab handles, where it is kept, and when it leaves the laboratory’s computer.

Who is responsible for patient data

The laboratory that uses MyLab decides what patient information is entered and how it is used. The laboratory is responsible for obtaining patient consent where required, controlling who can use MyLab, keeping its computers secure, and meeting applicable health-data laws. Reports produced by MyLab must be reviewed and authorised by a qualified professional before clinical use.

Data stored on the laboratory’s computer

  • Patient details (name, age, sex, contact number, address, referring doctor), test orders, results, reference ranges, reports, bills and payments, inventory, staff user accounts and an activity log.
  • This data is stored only on the computer or device where MyLab is installed, in that user’s MyLab data folder. Staff passwords are stored as one-way hashes.
  • The software publisher does not receive, view or collect this data, and MyLab contains no advertising, analytics or tracking.

When data leaves the computer

Data leaves the computer only when someone at the laboratory chooses to:

  • Export / backup to a file, saved wherever the user chooses.
  • Printing or saving a PDF of a report or bill.
  • Sharing a report via WhatsApp, which opens WhatsApp with the report text; WhatsApp’s own privacy policy then applies.
  • Optional cloud backup. If an administrator signs in to cloud backup (Settings > Data), a compressed copy of all MyLab data is uploaded over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection once a day to the MyLab backup service, hosted on Supabase. Each laboratory’s backups are stored in its own area and can only be read through that laboratory’s sign-in. The publisher operates this service and, as operator, has administrative access to its storage; the publisher does not access laboratory backups except where needed to keep the service running or when the laboratory asks for help. The 30 most recent backups are kept; older ones are deleted automatically. The laboratory can stop cloud backup at any time by signing out, and can ask for its backups to be deleted using the contact below.

Internet use

MyLab works without an internet connection. It uses the internet only for optional cloud backup, for links the user opens (such as WhatsApp), and for updates delivered by the Microsoft Store.

Retention and deletion

Data stays on the laboratory’s computer until the laboratory deletes it (Tools > Clear All Data) or deletes the MyLab data folder. Uninstalling MyLab does not delete the data folder, so that patient records are not lost by accident. Exported files are the laboratory’s responsibility to protect and delete.

Children

MyLab is used by laboratory staff, not by children. Laboratories may record patients of any age, including children, under their own responsibility.
